Pre-Buy FOMO Check (Rule 15)

Why do you want to buy now? Check and decide.

— Bad Reasons (FOMO)
It surged today and seems like it'll keep going up
→ FOMO. Wait one day.
Good news just came out
→ Lagging indicator. Check if already priced in.
A broker raised their price target
→ Lagging indicator. Not sufficient alone.
— Good Reasons
A pre-defined buy condition has been met
→ OK. Proceed with 3-tranche split entry.
Event result matches pre-set Scenario A
→ OK. First tranche entry possible.
